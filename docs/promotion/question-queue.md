# Question queue

Questions parents search for, in rough priority order. Roy's editor offers the next unchecked one, drafts an answer in Roy's voice from the site's researched content (federal law plus South Carolina where Roy has direct experience), and publishes after Roy approves. Titles are phrased as the parent would ask. Claude re-orders this weekly from search data. Tick a box only when the answer is live.

Rules for every answer: cite the law, regulation or the agency's own published page. No invented biography. No legal or medical advice. Clean language. No em dashes.

## The Diagnosis
- [ ] What do I do first after my child is diagnosed with autism?
- [ ] Do I need a diagnosis before my child can get services?
- [ ] How long does it take to get an autism evaluation, and can I speed it up?

## Birth to three
- [ ] What is early intervention and who qualifies?
- [ ] What is an IFSP and what should be in it?
- [ ] What happens when my child turns three and leaves early intervention?

## School years
- [ ] What is an IEP and what must it include?
- [ ] How do I request an evaluation from my child's school in writing?
- [ ] What can I do when I disagree with the IEP?
- [ ] What is the difference between an IEP and a 504 plan?
- [ ] What are my rights at an IEP meeting?
- [ ] What is prior written notice and when must the school give it?
- [ ] Can the school discipline or suspend my child with a disability?
- [ ] What is an independent educational evaluation and who pays for it?

## Paying for care
- [ ] Does my child qualify for SSI, and how does my income count?
- [ ] What is the difference between SSI and SSDI for a child?
- [ ] What is a Medicaid waiver and how long is the waitlist?
- [ ] My insurance denied therapy. How do I appeal?
- [ ] Does insurance have to cover ABA therapy?
- [ ] How do I keep a disabled child's benefits when someone leaves them money?
- [ ] What is an ABLE account and who can open one?
- [ ] What is a special needs trust and do I need one?

## Ages 14 to 26
- [ ] What should a transition plan include and when does it start?
- [ ] What changes at 18 for benefits, school and decisions?
- [ ] Do I need guardianship when my child turns 18, and what are the alternatives?
- [ ] When do I lose my child's health insurance coverage at 26?

## Reference and process
- [ ] How do I keep records so I can prove what the school or agency said?
- [ ] How do I write a letter that cites the rule and gets a response?
- [ ] Who can help me for free in South Carolina?
