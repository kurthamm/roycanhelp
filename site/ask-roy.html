Ask Roy

Parents ask me things. A lot of things. Some I know. Some I had to learn the hard way. Here's what I've learned from listening, and what keeps coming up again and again. Questions are published anonymously only if answered. Don't use your child's real name. No personal details.

Got a question?

Ask away. Your question is not posted publicly unless I pick it to answer, and if I do, I edit it and strip out anything identifying first. Please help me out by leaving that stuff off to begin with. See how I handle questions in the privacy policy.

Answers live on Roy's Wisdom. When I get to a question, it becomes a lesson on Roy's Wisdom where it belongs with the rest of what I've learned. Check there for what others have asked.

What happens to your question. It is stored on my server and read by me. It is not published unless I choose to answer it, and then only after I edit and anonymize it. To write a draft answer, the text of your question is sent to an AI service (Anthropic) that I use as a tool. This is not a confidential medical, legal, or benefits service, so please do not send anything you would not want a stranger to read. The details are in the privacy policy and the terms of use.

I'm a dad, not a doctor or a lawyer. Everything here is general information from my own experience and research. It is not medical, legal, tax, insurance, or benefits advice, and reading it does not create any professional relationship. Program rules, amounts, and deadlines change, and every state and every child is different. Verify anything that matters with the agency and a qualified professional before you act on it.
