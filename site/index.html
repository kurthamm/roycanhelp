My son was diagnosed with autism at three. My friend Kurt's daughter has Down syndrome. When our kids got their diagnoses, nobody handed us a manual. Just paperwork—so much paperwork. And a lot of waiting. In waiting rooms, on waiting lists, waiting for answers nobody seemed to have. So I started taking notes. I asked questions. I learned what actually works, what's worth fighting for, and what the bureaucracy doesn't want to tell you. Here's what I found.
