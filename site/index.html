My son was diagnosed with autism. When he got his diagnosis, nobody handed us a manual. Just paperwork: so much paperwork. And a lot of waiting. In waiting rooms, on waiting lists, waiting for answers nobody seemed to have. So I started taking notes. I asked questions. I learned what actually works, what's worth fighting for, and what the bureaucracy doesn't want to tell you. Here's what I found.
